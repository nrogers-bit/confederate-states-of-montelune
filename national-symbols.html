National Symbols

The official national symbols of the Confederate States of Montelune

National Identity

Current National Symbols

The national symbols of the Confederate States of Montelune represent the identity, history, and institutions of the Confederation.

National Animal

Golden Eagle

The Golden Eagle serves as the national animal of the Confederate States of Montelune.

It represents strength, independence, and national identity.

National Anthem

Badnerlied

Badnerlied serves as the national anthem of the Confederate States of Montelune.

The anthem is associated historically with Baden and is used by Montelune as a national ceremonial symbol.

National Flag

Flag of Montelune

Flag of the Confederate States of Montelune

The national flag serves as the principal visual symbol of the Confederate States of Montelune and is displayed on official government institutions and materials.
