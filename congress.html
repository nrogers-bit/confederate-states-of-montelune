Congress of Montelune

The Congress of the Confederate States of Montelune is the legislative institution of the Confederation.

Following the merger of the Congress and the former Roger Cabinet, Congress now combines legislative and administrative responsibilities within a single institution.

Members of Congress may hold specific areas of governmental responsibility while participating in the legislative affairs of the Confederation.

Congressional Administration

Members & Responsibilities
