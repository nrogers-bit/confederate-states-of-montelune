Government

The executive and legislative institutions of the Confederate States of Montelune

Government

Government Structure

The government of the Confederate States of Montelune is led by the President and Vice President, supported by the Roger Cabinet and the Congress of Montelune.

President

The President serves as the head of state and leads the executive administration of the Confederation.

Vice President

The Vice President serves alongside the President and assists in the administration of the Confederation.

Congress

Congress serves as the legislative institution of Montelune and is responsible for considering and passing legislation.

Executive Leadership

Presidential Administration

President of Montelune

Noah Rogers

Noah Rogers is a British-born micronational politician and noble who currently holds political offices in the Commonwealth of New Ridge and the Confederate States of Montelune, as well as a noble title in Vithuanum Vetus.

He currently serves as the Vice President of New Ridge, having taken office on 17 September 2026, after previously holding the office from 6 September 2026 to 8 September 2026, making him the 1st and 3rd Vice President of New Ridge.

Outside of New Ridge, he serves as the President of the Confederate States of Montelune, serving since 15 November 2025.

Vice President of Montelune

Shane Torres

Shane Torres, born 15 March 2012, known in Auritania as the Baron of Wyndhale and in Baustralia as the Viscount York, is an American-born micronationalist, military officer, and former royal.

He is notably known for founding the Republic of Berlandia, the Empire and Republic of Wallenderte, the Republic of the Northern Hills, and the United Commonwealth of Nova-Berlandia.

On 20 March 2026, he founded the Empire of New Greenia, where he served as Prime Minister for approximately two and a half months before resigning.

He is currently the President of the Commonwealth of New Ridge, having served since 6 September 2026.

Executive Administration

The Roger Cabinet

The Roger Cabinet shall consist of four Cabinet Secretaries, appointed to oversee the principal areas of government administration.

Cabinet Secretary I

Secretary of State

Shane Torres

The Secretary of State shall be responsible for foreign affairs, diplomatic relations, international cooperation, and the negotiation and administration of treaties and alliances.

Cabinet Secretary II

Secretary of the Treasury

Bad Wagon

The Secretary of the Treasury shall be responsible for the financial affairs of the Confederation, including the preparation of the national budget, taxation, government expenditure, and economic policy.

Cabinet Secretary III

Secretary of the Interior

Albrecht Jesus

The Secretary of the Interior shall be responsible for domestic affairs, internal administration, public safety, and matters concerning the administration and welfare of the States.

Cabinet Secretary IV

Secretary of Infrastructure and Development

hdford890

The Secretary of Infrastructure and Development shall be responsible for infrastructure, transportation, public development projects, and the continued development and improvement of the Confederation.

The Cabinet Secretaries shall serve under the authority of the President and Vice President and shall collectively advise the President on matters of national policy and administration.
Legislature

Congress of Montelune

Congress of the Confederate States of Montelune

Congress is the legislative institution of the Confederate States of Montelune. It is responsible for considering legislation, government measures, and other matters concerning the administration of the Confederation.
